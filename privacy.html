Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the controls you have over it — without the legalese fog.

← Back home About Contact Terms Privacy

1 What we collect

  • Account info — your email (or Google account) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Your account details — name, photo, and phone/WhatsApp, shown to members you message.
  • Listings you publish — an item's title, category, condition, location, starting price, description, and photos. All of this is public by design — see section 4.
  • Bids you place — the amount, timestamp, and your display name, attached to the item and visible in its public bid history.
  • Messages — the conversations you have with other members, so threads can be delivered and displayed.
  • Saved search filters — the price range, category, and condition you set on Browse, used to power Recommended and match notifications.
  • Activity — a "last active" timestamp shown as "active recently" to whoever you're chatting with, only if you've enabled it.
  • Reports & feedback — anything you report, or quick feedback you leave on a listing, so our team can review reports and other bidders can see feedback.
  • Notifications — a device token, only if you opt into browser push notifications.

2 What we don't collect

  • No GPS or precise location tracking — item location is only what you type in yourself.
  • No third-party ad trackers, and we don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't scan your messages to build an advertising profile of you.

3 How it's used

Your saved filters power Browse's Recommended section and match notifications — weighing price, category, and condition to surface listings worth a look. Messages and notifications exist to run the chat itself, and outbid/ending-soon alerts exist to run live bidding. Reports and feedback are used to keep listings honest and help other bidders decide. Your theme and accent-color choices are saved only in your own browser's local storage, never on our servers.

4 Nothing about a listing is hidden

Some auction sites hide the current price or bidder identities until you commit to bidding. VeusBid deliberately does the opposite: starting price, current bid, the full bid history, condition, contact info, and every photo on a listing are visible to any signed-in member, immediately, with no unlock step — because a fair auction depends on everyone seeing the same information.

5 Who can see what

  • Every live and closed auction — and its full bid history — is visible to any signed-in member.
  • Feedback left on a listing is visible to anyone viewing it, not just the owner.
  • Members you block can no longer show you their listings or message you.
  • "Active recently" status is visible to others only if you've turned that setting on.
  • Messages in a thread are visible only to the two people in it. They're transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored in our database — this isn't end-to-end encrypted, so please avoid sending anything you wouldn't want stored.

6 Where it's stored

VeusBid runs on Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest using Firebase's own storage encryption.

7 Your controls

  • Edit or delete any listing you've published, any time, from My Listings.
  • Toggle whether "active recently" is visible to others.
  • Block or report a member or listing directly from its detail view.
  • Delete your account entirely, any time, from Settings → Danger zone — this removes your profile and every listing you've published immediately (see the Danger zone for exactly what stays behind: past conversations remain with whoever you messaged, the same way deleting your number doesn't erase a friend's texts).

8 Notifications

If you enable browser push notifications, we use them to alert you to new messages, outbid alerts, auctions you're watching ending soon, and new listings that match your saved search. You can revoke this permission at any time through your browser's site settings.

9 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date below. Continuing to use VeusBid afterward means you accept the update.

10 Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at support@veusbid.app, or visit our contact page.
