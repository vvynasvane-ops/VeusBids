! Before you bid or pay, stay safe

  • Ask for extra photos or a video of the actual item before bidding on anything you can't inspect in person.
  • Keep early conversations with a seller on VeusBid rather than moving off-platform right away.
  • Never send payment outside the winning bid amount, or before an auction has actually ended — this is a common scam pattern.
  • If a listing looks fake or a seller makes you uncomfortable, use Block or Report. We review every report.
