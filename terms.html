By creating an account or otherwise using VeusBid, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree with them, please don't use the app. We'll note the date below whenever this page changes.

2 Who can use VeusBid

  • One account per person, under your own identity — no impersonating someone else.
  • Anyone can browse and message sellers; anyone can list an item they actually own or are authorized to sell.
  • Everything you publish on a listing — price, condition, location, photos — should be genuinely accurate, and every bid you place is a real commitment to buy if you win.

3 Your account & your listings

You're responsible for what happens under your account, and for keeping every listing you publish accurate up until the first bid lands — after that, editing is disabled so bidders can trust the listing they bid on. You're responsible for honoring bids you place: winning an auction is a commitment to complete the purchase. Nothing about a listing is hidden or locked behind a code; everything you publish, and every bid, is visible to every member on VeusBid.

4 Community conduct & safety

VeusBid exists to help people run honest, fast online auctions. You agree not to:

  • Publish a fake, duplicate, or already-sold listing, or misrepresent an item's condition, authenticity, or photos;
  • Place a bid you don't intend to honor if you win ("bid shielding" or shill bidding on your own listing included);
  • Harass, threaten, or deliberately deceive another member;
  • Solicit payment outside the agreed winning bid, or pressure a bidder before an auction has actually ended;
  • Post content that's sexually explicit, hateful, or promotes violence;
  • Use the app for unrelated advertising, spam, or commercial solicitation;
  • Attempt to access another member's account.

Ask for extra photos or details before bidding on anything you can't inspect in person, and keep early conversations with a seller on VeusBid. Use Block or Report on any listing or member that makes you uncomfortable — every report is reviewed by a person, not an algorithm.

You keep ownership of whatever you post — your listing photos, descriptions, bids, and messages. By posting it, you give VeusBid permission to store and display it within the app so the service can actually work. We can remove a listing, or restrict an account, that violates these terms.
